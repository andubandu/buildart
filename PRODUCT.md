# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Prospective apartment buyers and families seeking premium residential living in Tbilisi, Georgia. Visitors evaluate floor plans, apartment organization, balcony views, delivery timelines/status, and architectural quality to decide whether to book a viewing or inquire.

## Product Purpose
Buildart exists to showcase boutique residential developments in Tbilisi with uncompromising construction standards, well-organized living spaces, and real views. Success means presenting development credibility, architectural details, and floor plans clearly so qualified buyers convert into viewing appointments and apartment sales.

## Positioning
A boutique, high-craft real estate developer emphasizing thoughtful layouts, balcony views, and delivered/commissioned buildings in prime residential areas (such as Vake and Dighomi) rather than mass-market volume developments.

## Operating Context
- Residential real estate exploration in Tbilisi, Georgia.
- Users browse both high-level project overviews (facade renders, completion year, apartment count, floors, status) and granular unit specifications (detailed floor plan drawings, square meterage, layout dimensions).
- Bilingual market context: primarily Georgian language with English accessibility for international/diaspora buyers and investors.
- Contact and booking channel: direct inquiries and viewing bookings via contact details (buildart.office@gmail.com, social channels, phone).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Single-page application architecture built with Angular 22, TypeScript, SCSS, and HTML5.
- Project data served statically via `/json/projects.json` with associated high-resolution architectural renders, facade photography, and floor plan drawings in `/public/pictures/`.
- Project statuses include completed (`დასრულებული`), in-progress (`მიმდინარე`), and planned (`დაგეგმილი`).
- Horizontal deck navigation on the homepage with smooth scrollable project panels, side rail navigation, and responsive menu overlay.
- Undecided / Open: dynamic inquiry/booking submission form vs. direct contact links; full English/Georgian localization switcher toggle.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Buildart ("BUILDART").
- Tone of voice: Confident, refined, understated, architectural, grounded. Not loud, boastful, or generic real estate hype.
- Brand motto / copy commitment: "We build the best projects — for your comfort." / "Let's build something worth living in." / "ვაშენებთ, თქვენი კომფორტისთვის"
- Visual truth: Clean architectural presentation; let project renders and authentic floor plan drawings speak for the quality.

## Product Principles
1. **Architectural Truth Over Marketing Noise:** Highlight actual building specs, precise floor plans, and real renders rather than inflated claims.
2. **Clarity of Living Space:** Prioritize what matters most to residents — well-organized floor layouts, light, balcony views, and neighborhood context.
3. **Effortless Discovery:** Make exploring projects intuitive, fluid, and visual, leading smoothly from building overview to unit details to booking a viewing.
4. **Boutique Craftsmanship:** Every detail of the digital experience should reflect the care, permanence, and quality of the physical buildings.
